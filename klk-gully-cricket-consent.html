KLK · SEASON RULEBOOK

Gully Cricket

Read the full rulebook any time. Each team captain submits one consent for the whole tournament — confirming the squad has read and understood the rules below, including the latest updates.

Updated since the original rulebook

  • Corporate player mix (changed): the earlier caps of "max 1 corporate woman" and "max 2 corporate men + 1 corporate woman" no longer apply. The current rule is a flat maximum of 3 corporate players on the field at a time, in either combination: 2 corporate men + 1 corporate woman, or 2 corporate women + 1 corporate man.
  • No Impact Player rule: there is no impact-player substitution in this tournament. Once the playing XI (9 on field) is set for an innings per the fielding rules below, it stands.
  • Team changes window (new): new players can be added to a squad only while league matches are ongoing. Once the league stage ends, no further additions are allowed, and from the knockout stage onward no changes to a team's squad will be entertained at all.
Everything else below is unchanged from the original rulebook circulated to captains.
01 Squad & Team Composition
  • A squad can have a maximum of 15 players.
  • A squad must include a minimum of 2 women players.
  • On the field, 9 players play at a time, including at least 2 women players and one GenC player.
  • Of those 9, a maximum of 3 can be corporate players — either 2 corporate men + 1 corporate woman, or 2 corporate women + 1 corporate man. UPDATED
  • If a team has no woman associate present on the field, 24 runs (equivalent to one over, 12 runs/over) are awarded to the opponent.
  • A player can be part of only one team for the tournament.
  • A team may use any number of substitute fielders — fielding substitutes only.
  • New players can be added to a squad only during the league stage. No additions are allowed once league matches conclude, and no squad changes of any kind are permitted from the knockout stage onward. NEW
02 Match Basics
  • Each innings is 6 overs: 2 overs bowled to/by the women's segment, 4 overs for the men's segment.
  • This is box cricket — run-ups while bowling are not allowed.
  • All squads must be added to the Cricheros app; all scoring for the tournament is done through Cricheros.
  • Teams may bring and use their own kit.
  • A soft tennis ball is used throughout.
03 Playing Rules — Bowling & Batting
  • No bowler may bowl more than 1 over in an innings.
  • A batter who reaches 36 runs is retired, and can only come back in to bat after the 9th player.
  • If both women batters are out, that is treated as the end of two overs, and the innings resumes from the third over. "Last man standing" applies to the women's segment only.
  • Each innings gets one power-play over, chosen by the batting team. If the batting team doesn't nominate one, the last over of the innings automatically becomes the power play.
  • During the power play, only 7 fielders are on the field, and 2 of them must be women.
  • Men must not bowl with a full run-up or underarm action.
  • There is no restriction on bowling action for women.
  • Running between the wickets is allowed. Overthrows and byes count as valid runs; leg byes are capped at one run.
  • Every wide or no-ball costs one run plus an extra delivery.
  • The bowler must release the ball from inside the box — otherwise it's called as a no-ball.
04 Dismissals & Dead Balls
  • Every no-ball results in a free hit for the next delivery.
  • On a normal ball, a free hit, or a no-ball — a direct hit on the boundary, hitting either side grill, or the ball going to the back or outside the court results in the batter being out.
  • A ball that bounces more than once between the creases is a dead ball: no runs count, and an extra delivery is bowled.
05 Points & Standings
  • A win is worth 2 points; a loss is worth 0.
  • A drawn match is settled by a Super Over — repeated as many times as needed until there's a result.
  • Teams must report 30 minutes before the scheduled start. A team not present at the scheduled time concedes a walkover, and the opponent is awarded 2 points.
  • Group-stage qualification to the Pre-Quarter stage goes to the teams with the most points.
  • Ties on points after the group stage are broken in this order: first by head-to-head record between the tied teams, then by Net Run Rate (NRR).
06 Super Over Rules
  • A Super Over is a shortened match: one over (6 balls) per side, with 2 wickets per team.
  • Runs scored in a Super Over do not count toward a player's season stats.
  • The team that batted second in the original match bats first in the Super Over.
  • If the Super Over also ends level, another Super Over follows — this repeats until a winner is decided.
07 Match Day & Conduct
  • All teams report 30 minutes ahead of their scheduled game.
  • Once the toss is done, captains must decide bat or bowl immediately — no delays in that call.
  • If umpires judge that time will not allow a full 6 overs per side, they may shorten the overs for that match (applied to both innings) to keep the day's schedule on track.
  • The winner of each group advances to the knockout stage (Pre-Quarter).
  • The organizing committee reserves the right to amend rules whenever necessary.
  • Only the team captain may raise clarifications with the umpire. Players arguing with umpires may lead the organizing committee to cancel the match or apply a walkover against the team involved.
